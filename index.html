Project Overview

I develop high-fidelity, programmatic animations designed to build intuition around complex mathematical and physical systems. Operating entirely via Python Jupyter Notebooks, the rendering stack utilizes NumPy, Matplotlib, and OpenCV to generate educational videos, ranging from advanced chaotic systems to elementary fraction models.
